# Kur yra miestas? — Lithuania City Quiz

A small browser game: you get the name of a Lithuanian city and click where you think it is on the map. 10 rounds, up to 1000 points each — the closer your click, the more points.

**Play:** https://stasysgirkontas-create.github.io/lithuania-city-quiz/

## How to play
- Press **Start**, read the city name, click the map.
- Press **Next** (or Enter / Space) for the next city.
- Scoring: 1000 for a perfect click, ≈780 at 10 km off, ≈290 at 50 km off.
- High scores are saved in your own browser.

## Run it yourself
It's a single `index.html` file with no dependencies — just open it in a browser.
